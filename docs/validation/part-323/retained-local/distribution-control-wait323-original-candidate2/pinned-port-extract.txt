380: ## PORT_EXT
381: 
382: | 1     | N      | 4    | 1          |
383: | ----- | ------ | ---- | ---------- |
384: | `102` | `Node` | `ID` | `Creation` |
385: 
386: Same as [`NEW_PORT_EXT`](erl_ext_dist.md#new_port_ext) except the `Creation`
387: field is only one byte and only two bits are significant, the rest are to be 0.
388: 
389: ## NEW_PORT_EXT
390: 
391: | 1    | N      | 4    | 4          |
392: | ---- | ------ | ---- | ---------- |
393: | `89` | `Node` | `ID` | `Creation` |
394: 
395: Same as [`V4_PORT_EXT`](erl_ext_dist.md#v4_port_ext) except the `ID` field is
396: only four bytes. Only 28 bits are significant; the rest are to be 0.
397: 
398: `NEW_PORT_EXT` was introduced in OTP 19, but only to be decoded and echoed back.
399: Not encoded for local ports.
400: 
401: In OTP 23 distribution flag
402: [`DFLAG_BIG_CREATION`](erl_dist_protocol.md#DFLAG_BIG_CREATION) became
403: mandatory. All ports are now encoded using `NEW_PORT_EXT`, even external ports
404: received as [`PORT_EXT`](erl_ext_dist.md#port_ext) from older nodes.
405: 
406: ## V4_PORT_EXT
407: 
408: | 1     | N      | 8    | 4          |
409: | ----- | ------ | ---- | ---------- |
410: | `120` | `Node` | `ID` | `Creation` |
411: 
412: Encodes a port identifier (obtained from `erlang:open_port/2`). `Node` is the
413: originating node, [encoded as an atom](erl_ext_dist.md#utf8_atoms). `ID` is a
414: 64-bit big endian unsigned integer. The `Creation` works just like in
415: [`NEW_PID_EXT`](erl_ext_dist.md#new_pid_ext). Port operations are not allowed
416: across node boundaries.
417: 
418: In OTP 26 distribution flag [`DFLAG_V4_NC`](erl_dist_protocol.md#DFLAG_V4_NC) as
419: well as `V4_PORT_EXT` became mandatory accepting full 64-bit ports to be decoded
420: and echoed back.
