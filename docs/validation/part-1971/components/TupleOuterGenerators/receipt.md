# Tuple outer list-generator admission

## Frozen inputs and measured scope

The Part 1968 full rank contains 3,240 missing reference function keys in 366 modules. Comparing actual BEAM module keys from the Part 1969 independent preview and grouped-alias pilot, with the latter taking precedence for shared paths, covered 31 of those modules. It restored 21 previously missing keys, including 16 lc$^ keys in nine modules. The residual lc$^ spelling count is 1,904 keys in 311 modules and 998 source MFAs. A unique line-and-owner join to the pinned raw abstract forms identifies 1,689 of them as actual list-comprehension keys in 301 modules and 907 source MFAs.

Of the exact list-comprehension keys, 327 in 58 modules and 135 owner MFAs came from multiple generators. Exactly two ordinary generators occurred in 247 keys across 53 modules and 108 owners. The two ordinary generators with no filter, with variable or flat tuple fields, and variable/static-call sources comprised 64 keys in 18 modules and 28 owner MFAs. Restricting the later variable source to a binding from the outer pattern gave 20 keys in six modules and nine owner MFAs. Four keys in qlc_pt:mforms/1 and mforms/2 match the standalone flat-tuple gate. The other 16 require separate sibling sequencing, append or other pattern work. Counts described here measure previously missing function identity, not matching opcode bodies or matching whole files. The preview is partial and an unobserved module retains its Part 1968 rank classification.

## OTP proof and change

The OTP 29.1.1 v3_core lc_tq1 recursion creates both local definitions and nests the later letrec in the accepted outer generator clause. Pre-evaluation of the outer source happens once in the parent, before that first call. A failed outer tuple match skips without computing the inner source. The accepted outer arm evaluates that source immediately, on every accepted element, and transfers the outer tail to the inner helper. The inner empty arm resumes the outer helper at that tail. An invalid source or improper remainder reaches its own bad_generator arm and preserves the actual remainder.

Real pinned pre-optimization SSA and physical release entry order are preserved in qlc-pinned-ssa.stdout.log. The mforms/1 outer /1 calls inner /3 with source, outer tail and retained File. The mforms/2 outer /2 calls inner /3 with source, captured Tag and outer tail. Wider captures and whole-module sibling schedules need their own proof.

The patch reuses ExtractRecursiveListGenerators and BuildRecursiveMapping, covering exactly one ordinary no-filter two-generator expression in the existing unconditional-owner path. It admits a two-field flat tuple with fresh, distinct bindings and a variable inner pattern. The later source is a bound outer field or an existing supported static call, with at most one outer field carried through that loop. The existing variable-pattern branch remains under its original restrictions. The helper uses the existing capture and emission order, preserves tuple mismatch and captures, and attaches the measured nested call locations. Repeated variables stay on the old equality-bearing path.
