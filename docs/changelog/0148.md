## Part 1515 — 2026-11-13 — Narrow dispatch with finite atom arguments

**Part status:** verified on the release corpus, focused checks, and compiler block. **Objective and scope:** specialize exhaustive local dispatch when every incoming argument is covered by a finite atom set.

### Changes

The [argument proof](../../src/Compiler/Erlang.Compiler/Compilation/BeamModuleCompiler.FiniteAtomArguments.cs) follows direct callers, forwarded parameters, and prior bindings whose branches return atoms. The [label planner](../../src/Compiler/Erlang.Compiler/Compilation/BeamModuleCompiler.cs) and emitter share the reduced plan. Depending on the reachable arms, the emitter writes a constant return, an exact comparison, or a smaller table. The [permanent fixture](../../tests/Erlang.Tests/Resources/Beam/beam_finite_atom_probe.erl) covers literal and tail-call dispatch, unknown arguments, and the six-value case.

### Decisions and compatibility

The analysis uses the five-atom join limit from pinned OTP 29.1.1 `beam_types`. The initial build exceeded that limit and lost three matching bodies. The final build restored the three images to their previous hashes. It reuses the existing call and dispatch emitters and adds no dependency.
